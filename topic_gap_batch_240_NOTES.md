# Topic gap batch: 30 documents per category

This directory contains 240 staged candidate documents: 30 each for geography, earth/environment, space, human senses/perception, energy/power, social/institutional power, literature, and entertainment/performance.

All records retain source/revision provenance, content hashes, and the collector's `SOURCE_DECLARED_NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED` license state and `UNREVIEWED` source-quality state. Wikipedia revision history is retained per record. These documents have not been admitted to training.

The acquisition categories are discovery labels, not quality judgments. The pipeline preflight is only a structural/hash and heuristic screen; a human must still check relevance, article quality, item-level notices/attribution, factuality, and suitability. Several short pages are intentionally retained as candidates and will be flagged for review. This collection does not supply structured reasoning/epistemic examples or a held-out evaluation set.

Three language-focused pages were left out of the target count to honor the request to skip linguistics: `Interlingue literature`, `Literary language`, and `Stylistics`. Mathematics content was not collected.
